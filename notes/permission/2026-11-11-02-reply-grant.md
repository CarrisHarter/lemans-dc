Dear Marcus Ellwood,

Thank you for your email and for the clear description of your proposed project.

We are happy to grant you permission for the personal, non-commercial
preservation analysis of *Le Mans 24 Hours* (Sega Dreamcast, 2001 Japanese
release) as outlined in your request, subject to the following conditions:

1. You may create a personal backup/dump of a physical disc that you own solely
   for the purpose of this static analysis.
2. Static reverse engineering of the executable is permitted.
3. You may publish technical notes, recovered C-like pseudocode, named data
   structures, and documentation of file formats, provided that:
   - No ROM, disc image, extracted assets (art, audio, models, etc.), or any
     material that would allow the game to be played is distributed or made
     available.
   - The work remains strictly non-commercial.
   - You include a clear notice that the analysis is unofficial and that all
     rights in the game remain with the respective rights holders.

This permission covers only the rights we control in the title. It does not
extend to any third-party licences (vehicle manufacturers, sponsors, liveries,
trademarks, etc.). You remain solely responsible for ensuring you do not
infringe any such third-party rights.

We reserve the right to request that you stop the work and delete related
materials if we later determine that the project exceeds the scope of this
permission or becomes commercial in nature.

Please reply to confirm that you accept these terms. Once we have your
confirmation we will consider the permission granted.
