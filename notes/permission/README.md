## What was asked

Permission for personal, non-commercial preservation analysis of
*Le Mans 24 Hours* (Sega Dreamcast, 2001, Japanese release): dumping a disc the
author owns, statically reverse engineering the executable, and publishing
technical notes, recovered C-like pseudocode, named data structures and file
format documentation — with no ROM, disc image or extracted asset distributed,
non-commercially, and with an unofficial-analysis notice.

## What was granted

Permission granted on those terms, with no further formalities. Conditions:

1. a personal backup/dump of a physical disc the author owns, solely for this analysis;
2. static reverse engineering of the executable;
3. publication of notes/pseudocode/structures/format docs, provided no ROM,
   disc image, extracted assets or anything enabling play is distributed, the
   work stays strictly non-commercial, and the notice below is included.

Carve-out: covers only rights Atari SA controls; third-party licences (vehicle
manufacturers, sponsors, liveries, trademarks) are excluded and remain the
author's responsibility.

Revocability: Atari SA reserves the right to require the work to stop and
materials to be deleted if it exceeds scope or becomes commercial.

## Approved attribution wording (verbatim)

> This technical analysis is unofficial and is not endorsed by or affiliated with
> Atari SA or any of its licensors. All rights in *Le Mans 24 Hours* remain with
> their respective owners.

Placement: at the beginning of published notes, or on a dedicated
credits/acknowledgements page. No obligation to send publication to Atari SA; a
link for their records is welcome.
