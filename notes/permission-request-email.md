# Permission request — email draft

Save/edit before sending. Placeholders in `[square brackets]`.

## Who to send it to

| recipient | why | how to find the address |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Atari SA** (successor to Infogrames, publisher of the 2001 releases) — Business Affairs / Legal | owns the publisher-side rights to the title | Atari's site contact form, or `legal@`/`business@` on their published domain; ask for routing to Business Affairs |
| **Sega Europe / Sega of Japan — Licensing** | the Japanese Dreamcast release was distributed through Sega | Sega's licensing contact page; mention the Japanese SKU |
| **ACO (Automobile Club de l'Ouest)** — media/licensing | licenses the "24 Heures du Mans" name and marks | ACO media/licensing contact form |

Send to publisher and Sega separately rather than one mass email; cc'ing ACO is optional and
usually slows things down. Attach nothing except, if you have one, a photo/receipt proving you
own a physical copy — that materially improves the odds.

Note: this title also carries per-manufacturer and sponsor licences (cars, liveries, brands).
A publisher's "yes" may not cover all of it, so ask for the scope explicitly, as the draft does.

---

**Subject:** Permission request — non-commercial preservation analysis of *Le Mans 24 Hours* (Sega Dreamcast, 2001)

Dear [name / Business Affairs team],

I am writing to ask permission for a personal, non-commercial preservation project involving
*Le Mans 24 Hours* as released for the Sega Dreamcast in 2001 (Japanese release, product number
[XX-XXXXX]).

**What I would like to do.** Produce a documented, human-readable description of how the game
works — recovered C-like pseudocode for individual routines, named data structures, and notes on
the file formats used on the disc. This is static analysis of a game I own; it involves no
modification of the game, no online play, and no distribution of the game itself.

**What I would not do.** I would not distribute the ROM, disc image, or any extracted art, audio,
models or other assets, in any form. I would not publish anything that lets someone play the game
without buying it. Findings would be limited to technical description: function behaviour, struct
layouts, format documentation.

**What I am asking for.** Written confirmation that this analysis is acceptable to you, and
clarification of its scope, specifically:

1. that making a personal backup/dump of a disc I own for this purpose is acceptable;
2. that static reverse engineering of the executable is acceptable;
3. whether publishing my technical notes and pseudocode (no assets, no ROM) is acceptable, or
   whether you would prefer the notes stay private.

If it helps, I am happy to sign a confidentiality agreement, to share drafts before anything is
published, to credit or acknowledge any rights holders you specify, and to stop and delete the work
at your request.

If this is not something you can grant, I would be grateful for a short reply saying so, or for a
pointer to whoever can decide.

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
[full name]
[city, country]
[email] · [phone, optional]
[link to project notes, optional]
