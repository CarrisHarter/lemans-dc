## Scope of permission

Permission was requested for personal, non-commercial preservation analysis of a
physical disc owned by the author. Business Affairs, Atari SA replied on
2026-11-11 granting it, and on the author's acceptance confirmed permission
granted with no further formalities, subject to conditions:

1. a personal backup/dump of an owned physical disc, solely for this analysis;
2. static reverse engineering of the executable;
3. publication of technical notes, pseudocode, named structures and format
   documentation — with no ROM/disc image/assets, non-commercial only, and the
   approved attribution notice shown above.

It covers only the rights that publisher controls; third-party licences
(vehicle manufacturers, sponsors, liveries, trademarks) are excluded, and the
permission may be withdrawn, in which case the work stops and materials are
deleted. No copy of publication needs to be sent to them; a link is welcome for
their records.
